Morning in Amsterdam

Amsterdam wakes up slowly with the sound of bicycles moving across old brick streets and boats passing quietly through the canals. The city is famous for its beautiful canal system, narrow houses, and historic bridges that connect different parts of the city together.

The canals were originally built for transportation, trade, and water management. Over time, they became one of the most important parts of Amsterdam’s identity. Today, the city combines history with modern culture, making it one of the most unique urban spaces in Europe.
